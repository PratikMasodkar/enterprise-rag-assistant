Company Remote Work and Travel Policy

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Employees must remain available during normal working hours.

Employees are required to attend scheduled meetings.

Travel Policy

Business class travel is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours.

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel.

Accommodation expenses can be claimed up to 6000 rupees per night.

Learning and Development Policy

Employees receive an annual learning budget of 15000 rupees.

The budget may be used for approved professional courses and certifications.

Manager approval is required before using the learning budget.
